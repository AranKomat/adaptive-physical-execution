not an exact pad mesh or contact certificate. In the original wrist camera,

## Separate exploratory contact test

After explicitly ending the visual-gate trial, the operator chose a separate
simulator-only closure probe under standing bounded-experiment authorization.
This is a protocol change, NOT the reviewer changing its decision or the visual
gate passing. Unknown clearance was explicit in the request and user update.
The probe held the current hand pose and commanded opening fraction 0.3 for
at most 64 actions. No lift was bundled with closure; no paid call was needed.

Closure ran 64 actions / 4.267 simulated seconds / 30.339 wall seconds. Final
position error was 2.354 mm and orientation error 0.040974 rad, exceeding the
existing 0.03 rad arrival criterion. It stopped without retry or lift. This is
a nonarrival result, not a hard 0.35 rad orientation-abort event and not proof of
dangerous contact. Grasp assistance remains ON. Secure capture was not verified.

## Next decision

Stop further static camera sweeps. Framing is now measurably better, but contact
geometry and contact-stage tracking remain unqualified. Distinguish the nominal
surface-selected point from the center between opposing contact surfaces; a
continuous depth patch alone is insufficient. Next test should use observed
opposing-surface geometry and a predeclared contact-stage protocol, preserving
raw errors and separating arrival tolerance from hard abort limits. Do not
retroactively relax this probe's tolerance to call it successful. Full assembly,
autonomous recovery and experience reuse remain open.
